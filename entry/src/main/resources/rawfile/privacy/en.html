REPS Privacy Policy

Effective: 1 November 2026 · Updated: 26 October 2026 · Version 1

This policy explains how REPS ("the app") handles your personal information. The app is developed and operated by Zhang Yichang ("we"). The Chinese version of this policy prevails.

1. In one sentence

Without signing in, the app never goes online and all data stays on your device. Only after you choose to sign in with a HUAWEI ID does the app sync the study data listed below to cloud services provided by HUAWEI AppGallery Connect, so you can use it across your own devices.

We do not collect your phone number, contacts, location, photos or hardware identifiers (the device ID is random and unrelated to the hardware), and we do not upload your individual answer history. The app requests only the network permission, and uses it only after you sign in.

3. Third-party services

Sync uses HUAWEI AppGallery Connect services from Huawei Technologies Co., Ltd.: Auth Service, Cloud DB, Cloud Storage and Account Kit (SDKs: @hw-agconnect/auth, Cloud Foundation Kit, Account Kit). Huawei's processing is governed by Huawei's developer services privacy statement. We never sell or share your information with anyone else.

4. Storage location and retention

Cloud data is stored in mainland China and kept while your account exists. When you delete your account we delete all of your cloud sync data and deck files. On-device data is removed when you uninstall the app or clear it in the app.

5. Your rights

  • Every study feature works without signing in.
  • Sign out any time under Me → Account; syncing stops and your on-device data stays.
  • Delete your account any time under Me → Account, which deletes your cloud data [remove this note once account deletion ships].
  • Export any deck as a YAML file; exports contain only card content, never study history.
  • To access, correct or delete your information, or withdraw consent, contact us below; we reply within 15 working days.

6. Children

If you are under 14, please use sign-in and sync only with a guardian's consent and guidance.

7. Changes

We will notify you in the app when this policy changes, and ask for consent again for material changes to what we process.
